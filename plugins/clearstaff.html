Why ClearStaff

  • One-click punishments: /punish opens a menu of your own warn, mute, kick, temp ban and ban presets.
  • Logged history: every punishment goes to history.yml, and /history shows it.
  • Uses the vanilla ban list: temp bans and bans have an expiry and /pardon works as normal.

Features

  • /vanish hides you from the player list and the world, with optional fake quit and join messages
  • /freeze blocks movement and commands (with a whitelist) and damage, and tells staff if a frozen player logs out
  • /invsee and /endersee, read-only unless you have clearstaff.invsee.edit
  • Staff chat: /sc <message>, or /sc on its own to toggle
  • Mutes handled by the plugin, with /unmute
  • Separate permissions for every tool, commented config.yml, MiniMessage text

Commands & permissions

CommandPermission
/vanish (/v)clearstaff.vanish; see vanished staff with clearstaff.vanish.see
/freeze <player>clearstaff.freeze

All permissions default to op. clearstaff.* gives everything.

Tested

Example config

# ClearStaff. All text is MiniMessage. Punishments are logged to history.yml.
prefix: "<dark_gray>[<light_purple>Staff</light_purple>]</dark_gray> "
vanish:
  # Broadcast a fake quit message when you vanish and a fake join message when you reappear.
  fake-messages: true
  fake-quit: "<yellow><player> left the game"
  fake-join: "<yellow><player> joined the game"
freeze:
  # Seconds between reminder messages to a frozen player.
  message-every: 5
  message: "<red><bold>You are frozen.</bold> Do not log out. Wait for staff instructions."
  # Commands a frozen player may still use (without the slash).
  allowed-commands: [msg, r, tell, helpop]
staff-chat:
  format: "<light_purple>[SC]</light_purple> <white><player></white><gray>:</gray> <message>"
mute-message: "<red>You are muted<expiry>. Reason: <reason>"
# Punishment menu presets, shown in this order in /punish <player>.
# type: warn, mute, kick, tempban or ban. duration (mute/tempban): e.g. 30m, 1h, 1d, 7d.
presets:
  warn:
    type: warn
    icon: PAPER
    name: "<yellow>Warn"
    reason: "Breaking the rules"
  mute1h:
    type: mute
    icon: BOOK
    name: "<gold>Mute 1 hour"
    duration: 1h
    reason: "Chat abuse"
  kick:
    type: kick
    icon: LEATHER_BOOTS
    name: "<gold>Kick"
    reason: "Please read the rules"
  tempban1d:
    type: tempban
    icon: IRON_SWORD
    name: "<red>Temp ban 1 day"
    duration: 1d
    reason: "Griefing"
  ban:
    type: ban
    icon: BARRIER
    name: "<dark_red>Permanent ban"
    reason: "Cheating"
messages:
  warned: "<red>You have been warned: <reason>"
  broadcast: "<light_purple><staff></light_purple> <gray>gave</gray> <white><player></white> <gray>a</gray> <white><action></white> <gray>(<reason>)</gray>"

Installation

Full walkthrough: ClearStaff setup guide.

  1. Stop your server.
  2. Put ClearStaff-1.0.0.jar in your plugins/ folder.
  3. Start the server so plugins/ClearStaff/config.yml is created.
  4. Edit the freeze whitelist and punishment presets in the config, then run /clearstaff reload.
  5. Give your staff the permissions they need (all default to op).
