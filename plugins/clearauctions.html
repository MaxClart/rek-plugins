Why ClearAuctions

  • One-click buying: a paged 54-slot menu shows each item as it really is, with seller, price and time left. Sellers are paid straight away, online or offline.
  • Expiry and collection box: listings expire after a time you set (48 hours by default) and the item goes back to the seller's /ah collect box.
  • Built against duplication: every click type and drag is cancelled, clicks are rate-limited and re-checked, the listing is removed and saved before money or items move, and failed payments are rolled back.

Features

  • /ah sell <price> lists the whole stack in your hand
  • Sort by newest, lowest price, highest price or ending soon, and /ah search <text>
  • Cancel your own listing from "Your listings" or with /ah cancel <id>
  • Max listings per player, optional flat or percentage listing fee, optional sales tax, min and max price
  • Material blacklist and blocked-names list
  • Data saved in auctions.yml after every transaction
  • Switches itself off with a clear console message if no economy plugin is found

Tested

Tested on Paper 1.21.4 with three bots, Vault 1.7.3 and EssentialsX 2.22.0: about 100 clicks of every type plus drag packets on one listing bought it exactly once with nothing duplicated; two buyers clicking at once gave it to one and charged one; buying while the seller cancelled (three ways) let exactly one action through; an enchanted, renamed sword with lore and damage sold intact; sorting, search, the max-listings limit, the blacklist, the listing fee, blocked self-buying and cancelling from "Your listings" worked; a listing expired on time and came back through /ah collect; listings and the collection box survived a restart; money added up to the cent; and with no economy plugin it disabled itself with a clear message. Not tested: paging past 45 listings, the sales tax, the percentage fee, the blocked-names list, a full inventory sending a bought item to the collection box, a payment failure rollback, and economy plugins other than EssentialsX.

Example config

# ClearAuctions. Needs Vault and an economy plugin (e.g. EssentialsX); without one the plugin disables itself.

# Max active listings per player (clearauctions.bypass.limit ignores this).
max-listings: 5
# Price limits for one listing (the whole stack).
min-price: 0.01
max-price: 1000000
# How long a listing stays up, in minutes (2880 = 48 hours). Unsold items go to the seller's collection box.
listing-duration-minutes: 2880
# How often to check for expired listings, in seconds.
expiry-check-seconds: 10
# Fee taken from the seller when listing (not refunded on cancel or expiry). Flat amount plus a percent of the price.
listing-fee: 0
listing-fee-percent: 0
# Percent of the sale price kept back from the seller when an item sells (0 = seller gets the full price).
sales-tax-percent: 0
# Milliseconds between clicks in the menu (stops click spam).
click-cooldown-ms: 300
# Materials that can't be listed.
blacklist: [BEDROCK, BARRIER, COMMAND_BLOCK, CHAIN_COMMAND_BLOCK, REPEATING_COMMAND_BLOCK, STRUCTURE_BLOCK, JIGSAW, DEBUG_STICK, LIGHT]
# Block items whose name contains any of these words (case-insensitive). Leave empty to allow all names.
blacklist-name-contains: []
currency: "$"
messages:
  prefix: "<dark_gray>[<gold>AH</gold>]</dark_gray> "

Installation

Full walkthrough: ClearAuctions setup guide.

  1. Stop your server.
  2. Put ClearAuctions-1.0.0.jar in your plugins/ folder.
  3. Start the server so plugins/ClearAuctions/config.yml is created.
  4. Install Vault and an economy plugin (e.g. EssentialsX) first.
  5. Edit max listings, duration, fees, tax, price limits and the blacklist in the config, then run /ah reload.
