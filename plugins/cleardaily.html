ClearDaily

Give players a reason to log in every day. They type /daily, see the whole calendar, and click today's reward. Tomorrow they move on to the next day.

Why ClearDaily

  • Clear calendar: claimed days, today's glowing reward and locked days are all marked.
  • Real streaks: miss a whole day and it's back to day 1 (or turn that off).
  • Your timezone: the day rolls over at midnight in the timezone you set.

Features

  • Up to 45 days, each with its own icon, name, items and console commands (%player%)
  • Loop back to day 1 after the last day, or stop (configurable)
  • Join reminder when a reward is waiting (can be turned off)
  • Streak card showing total rewards claimed and time until the next one
  • Progress saved to data.yml, so it survives restarts
  • Full inventories: leftover items drop at the player's feet, never lost
  • MiniMessage colours in all names and messages

Commands & permissions

CommandPermission
/daily (or /rewards)cleardaily.use (everyone by default)
/daily reset <player>cleardaily.admin (ops by default)
/daily setday <player> <day>cleardaily.admin
/daily reloadcleardaily.admin

None.

Example config

# ClearDaily - a daily login rewards calendar.
# Players claim one reward per day. Claiming on consecutive days moves them along the calendar.
timezone: "Europe/London"     # when the "day" rolls over
reset-on-miss: true           # missing a whole day sends the player back to day 1
loop: true                    # after the last day, start again from day 1
remind-on-join: true          # tell players on join when a reward is waiting
messages:
  prefix: "<gold>[Daily]</gold> "
  claimed: "<green>You claimed your day <day> reward!"
  already: "<yellow>You've already claimed today's reward. Come back in <time>."
  waiting: "<green>Your day <day> reward is ready! Type <white>/daily</white> to claim it."
  streak-lost: "<red>You missed a day, so your streak went back to day 1."
  finished: "<gray>You've claimed every reward on the calendar."
  inventory-full: "<yellow>Your inventory was full, so some items dropped at your feet."
gui:
  title: "<gold><bold>Daily Rewards"
# Up to 45 days. Unlisted day numbers in between are skipped.
days:
  1: {icon: BREAD, name: "<white>16 Bread", items: [{material: BREAD, amount: 16}]}
  2: {icon: IRON_INGOT, name: "<white>8 Iron Ingots", items: [{material: IRON_INGOT, amount: 8}]}
  3: {icon: GOLD_INGOT, name: "<yellow>$250", commands: ["eco give %player% 250"]}
  4: {icon: EXPERIENCE_BOTTLE, name: "<green>16 Bottles o' Enchanting", items: [{material: EXPERIENCE_BOTTLE, amount: 16}]}
  5: {icon: GOLDEN_APPLE, name: "<gold>2 Golden Apples", items: [{material: GOLDEN_APPLE, amount: 2}]}
  6: {icon: EMERALD, name: "<green>8 Emeralds", items: [{material: EMERALD, amount: 8}]}
  7: {icon: DIAMOND, name: "<aqua>3 Diamonds + $1,000", items: [{material: DIAMOND, amount: 3}], commands: ["eco give %player% 1000"]}

Requirements

  • Paper 1.21 (tested on 1.21.4; other 1.21.x Paper builds are expected to work)
  • Java 21
  • Folia is not supported yet

Installation

  1. Stop your server.
  2. Put ClearDaily-1.0.0.jar in your plugins/ folder.
  3. Start the server so plugins/ClearDaily/config.yml is created.
  4. Set your timezone and the rewards for each day in the config, then run /daily reload.
