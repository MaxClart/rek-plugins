ClearAuctions setup guide

Set up a single-server auction house with sort, search, expiry and a collection box.

Buy ClearAuctions — $7.99 Plugin page

1. Install

Follow the getting started guide: put ClearAuctions-1.0.0.jar in plugins/, restart, and check the console. The config is created at plugins/ClearAuctions/config.yml.

2. Install Vault and an economy

ClearAuctions needs Vault and an economy plugin such as EssentialsX. Without one, it disables itself and says so in the console.

3. Config

Edit plugins/ClearAuctions/config.yml (max listings, duration, fees, tax, price limits, blacklist), then run /ah reload. Data is kept in plugins/ClearAuctions/auctions.yml and saved after every transaction. Single server only: don't point two servers at the same file.

4. How players use it

  • Hold an item and run /ah sell 100 to list the whole stack for $100. Custom, renamed and enchanted items keep their full data.
  • /ah opens the menu. Click to buy. /ah search sword filters, and /ah mine shows your listings (click to cancel).
  • /ah collect gives back items from expired or cancelled listings, or bought items that didn't fit.
  • /ah cancel <id> cancels a listing. Admins can cancel any, and the item goes back to the seller.

Aliases: /auction, /auctions.

5. Permissions

  • clearauctions.use: everyone by default.
  • clearauctions.admin: reload and cancel any listing. Ops by default.
  • clearauctions.bypass.limit and clearauctions.bypass.fee: no one by default, not even ops.

What was tested?

Duplication attempts with every click type, simultaneous buyers, buying while cancelling, enchanted items, sorting, search, limits, blacklist, listing fee, expiry, collection box, restarts and the no-economy shutdown were tested with three bots on Paper 1.21.4 with Vault and EssentialsX. Not tested: paging past 45 listings, the sales tax, the percentage fee, the blocked-names list, a full inventory sending a bought item to the collection box, a payment failure rollback, and economy plugins other than EssentialsX.
