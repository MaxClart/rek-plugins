ClearBosses setup guide

Make your own boss, give it phases and loot, and spawn it for an event.

Buy ClearBosses — $9.99 Plugin page

1. Install

Follow the getting started guide: put ClearBosses-1.0.0.jar in plugins/, restart, and check the console. The config is created at plugins/ClearBosses/config.yml.

2. Make a boss

Open plugins/ClearBosses/config.yml and add a boss under bosses:. The commented Zombie King and Witch Queen examples show every option: mob type, name, health, damage, speed, knockback resistance, equipment, boss bar range, phases, loot and rewards. Then run /boss reload.

3. Phases

Each phase triggers at a health percentage with a message and actions:

  • minions <TYPE> <count>
  • effect <EFFECT> <seconds> <level>
  • knockback <radius> <strength>
  • damage <radius> <amount>
  • lightning <radius>
  • heal <amount>

4. Spawn and manage

  • /boss spawn zombie_king spawns at the block you're looking at. Add here for your feet, or <world> <x> <y> <z> (works from console).
  • /boss list shows alive bosses with health and location.
  • /boss kill all removes them.

All commands need clearbosses.admin (ops by default). Minions stay alive after their boss dies until they're killed or the server stops.
